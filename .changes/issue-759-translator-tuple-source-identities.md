Interned anonymous tuple layouts retain actual Rust primitive, reference and ownership qualifiers, including nested fields, instead of sharing layouts solely by portable widths or scalar encodings. Whole-program adversarial checks reject earlier-declaration laundering of `usize` into `u64` and owned `String` into borrowed `str`; owned tuple construction remains supported. Checked semantic serialization and JavaScript/strict TypeScript execution retain the distinct layouts. This does not establish general Rust borrow checking or complete runtime parity.
