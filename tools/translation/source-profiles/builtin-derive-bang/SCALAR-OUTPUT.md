with one to five boolean or integer fields. It uses the unchanged full Clone,
Debug, generic derive, AST schema and builder members already audited here.

as references to unchanged licensed archives in sibling profiles. Previous
manifest rows and all original Router Rust/Cargo inputs remain unchanged.

preserves `kw::DollarCrate` with its definition-site context. It does not replace
that source identity with a guessed core/std runtime path. Source side format

route and the simple type-only assertions plus self dereference route. All
